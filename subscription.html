Absolutely. All payments are processed by Razorpay with 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store card details on our servers.
QCan I switch plans mid-cycle?
Yes. Upgrading takes effect immediately; you'll be charged the prorated difference. Downgrading applies at the end of the current period.

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), all major credit/debit cards, net banking, and EMI options — all via Razorpay.
